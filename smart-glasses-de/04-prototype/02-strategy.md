# Positioning Strategy: Smart Energy Storage Management System (Australia)

## 1. Product Positioning

**Positioning Statement**: For Australian battery-equipped households and energy retailers/installers, our smart energy storage management system is a hardware-agnostic AI dispatch and VPP orchestration software layer that turns passive storage into automated tariff and grid-value optimization without requiring new battery hardware or locking users into a single brand.

- **Who We Are**: A pure-software AI dispatch and orchestration layer (no hardware manufacturing or hardware price wars).
- **Who We Serve**: Existing solar + battery homeowners and electricity retailers/installers operating in Australia.
- **Problem Solved**: Batteries operating in basic self-consumption leave tariff arbitrage and VPP participation untapped, while single-brand apps create lock-in and limited cross-fleet visibility.
- **How We Differ**: Cross-brand software intelligence focused on economic optimization and B2B2C/white-label enablement rather than hardware margins.
- **Data Gap Note**: Granular conversion economics, OEM API access terms across specific battery models, and exact retailer VPP revenue-share benchmarks lack verified figures in `01-research.md` — *Insufficient data - recommend follow-up investigation.*

## 2. Target Segments

1. **Priority 1 — Existing Battery Owners Seeking Smarter Dispatch (Direct Subscription / App)**: Homeowners who already have solar and storage running basic self-consumption can adopt software optimization without waiting on new hardware subsidies or replacing existing equipment, directly addressing bill reduction and automated charging/discharging.
2. **Priority 2 — Electricity Retailers & Installers (B2B SaaS / White-Label B2B2C)**: Retailers managing VPP fleets and flexible load benefit from software orchestration and customer engagement, while installers can add value without competing purely on hardware price. Partner channels help overcome consumer reliance on trusted local installers and retailers identified in `01-research.md`.
3. **Secondary / Deferred**: Commercial & Industrial (C&I) users are deferred to a later phase pending stronger ROI proof and tailored load-forecasting validation; community/social housing VPP programs have *Insufficient data - recommend follow-up investigation* for near-term commercial prioritization.

## 3. Differentiation Strategy

| Competitor / Alternative | Their Approach | Our Differentiation Wedge |
|---|---|---|
| Battery & Inverter OEMs (e.g., Tesla, Sigenergy, Sungrow, SolarEdge, Redback) | Hardware + native ecosystem apps; strong installer familiarity but brand lock-in and hardware margin pressure | **Multi-brand software layer**: Upgrades existing batteries across brands and offers white-label/software value without forcing hardware replacement |
| Software & DER/VPP Platforms (e.g., SwitchDin, Amber-style dynamic/VPP models) | Cloud DER connectivity, telemetry, or dynamic/VPP optimization | **Pragmatic dual-track entry**: Pairs direct consumer optimization with retailer/installer B2B2C distribution and clear tariff/VPP ROI focus |

## 4. Go-to-Market Plan (GTM)

- **Phase 1 — Validation & Pilot Wedge**: Partner with select progressive solar/battery installers and pilot households to validate cross-brand connectivity, tariff/VPP dispatch logic, and real-world savings.
- **Phase 2 — B2B2C Channel & Subscription Expansion**: Offer white-label/API integration or co-marketed bundles with electricity retailers and installer networks alongside direct consumer subscription onboarding.
- **Phase 3 — Scale & Adjacent Expansion**: Expand supported battery/inverter compatibility and evaluate C&I peak-demand optimization once core residential and retailer unit economics are proven.
